## Decisions Made
- Used t-distribution CI (not z-interval) for mean_ci since it handles small n correctly (e.g., 5 CV folds)
- Wilson score interval chosen over Wald for proportions (better coverage at extreme values and small samples)
- Exact Poisson CI uses chi-squared method rather than normal approximation (correct for low counts)
- Copied _haversine_km into network_distances.py rather than importing from spatial_analysis.py or compute_lcogs.py to avoid fragile cross-module dependencies
- Self-treated patients (cod_ibge == cod_ibge_res) have distance=0, excluded from referred-patient distance statistics but included in total procedure count
